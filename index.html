About Me

I'm an AI Engineer at SPAN Technology, where I build custom MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers used across multiple AI clients — Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and Cline — with a multi-transport design (SSE and the native MCP protocol) that's extensible to new integrations.

I also work on an AI Copilot that combines an OpenAI-based chatbot with Anthropic Claude for agentic workflows. I migrated its vector store from Pinecone to PostgreSQL/pgvector and added Langfuse for LLM observability — that work has contributed to a 35% reduction in filing errors.

Currently, I'm building a document intelligence pipeline for tax form processing (1042-S and W-9), pairing AWS Textract for structured OCR with Google Gemini for complex, unstructured extraction — a system that has processed 10,000+ documents in production.
